Projects / GeoWimble

Geography and history games · Android

Know your world. Play it.

GeoWimble is a collection of quick geography and history games. Draw a circle that holds 10 million people, name a flag before the run is over, recognise a country from its outline and take on a new Daily Challenge every day. Free to play, with no account.

Get GeoWimble on Google Play ↗ Back to all projects

No account · Map and flag games work offline · Progress stays on your device

One more round.

Every game takes a minute or two. Place a pin on a city, split a country in two equal halves, tap every neighbour of a country or guess which of two historical photos is older. Streak games keep going until you slip, so there is always a reason for one more round.

The map and flag games work offline, and only the history photo games need a connection, so GeoWimble fits a short break as well as a long evening of chasing your best streak.

01

Flag Sudden Death

A random flag, and one wrong answer ends the run. Pick from four choices, or switch to open answers and type the country name yourself.

02

Draw, place and guess

Draw a circle around a given number of people, split a country in two equal halves and guess how many people live inside an area.

03

Countries and cities

Recognise a country from its outline, find countries on the map, drop a pin on a city and name the neighbours of a country.

04

Daily Challenge

Five tasks that are the same for everyone, once a day. One scored attempt, so a good day is worth remembering.

05

Streaks and puzzles

Higher or lower comparisons, Geography Connections puzzles and the One-Minute World race against the clock.

06

History in photos

Which of two historical photos is older, and in which year was this photo taken? A change of pace between the map games.

Calm by design.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress is stored on your device, and the map and flag games work offline. Ads are served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where it is required, and purchases go through Google Play.

Ads stay out of the way: optional reward ads for extra plays, a short ad only after a finished game and a banner on menu screens, never while you are guessing.
