Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what happens with information when you use GeoWimble: Geography Games. Lunava Software (sole proprietorship, KvK 42195099), Weg acker 47, 1965 TG Heemskerk, the Netherlands is responsible for the app. Contact: lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

The short version

  • GeoWimble has no account and no server of ours. Your scores, records, history, daily challenge results and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, a full-screen ad between some games and optional rewarded videos that give extra plays. Google may process some device information for that, after you give consent where the law requires it.
  • The two history photo games load photos live from the NASA Image and Video Library and the Library of Congress. Those servers see your IP address when a photo is requested.
  • Purchases (Remove ads, GeoWimble Pro) are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your progress is deleted. We cannot restore it.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or have access to your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores on your device: your records and statistics per game, your game history, daily challenge results and streak, achievements, how many free games you used today, bonus plays from rewarded ads, your settings (sound, vibration, theme, practice options, how you sort the game list) and your purchase status (for example Remove ads or Pro). You can remove it by using Settings > Delete all data, by clearing the app's data in Android or by uninstalling the app.

Settings > Export backup creates a file on your device that you choose where to save or share. We never receive that file. It does not contain your purchases.

3. Photos from NASA and the Library of Congress

The games "Older or Newer" and "Guess the Year" show historical photographs that are in the public domain or free to use. The photos are not stored in the app; they are loaded from images-assets.nasa.gov (NASA), www.loc.gov and tile.loc.gov (Library of Congress) when you play these games. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address, the type of device and the photo you requested. We do not send them anything else and do not control their logs. All other games work offline.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA/UK; Google LLC elsewhere). Ads appear as a banner on calm screens (never during a game), as a full-screen ad between some games and as optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for extra plays. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app version and interactions with ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. In other regions, ads may be requested as allowed by local law; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If you buy "Remove ads" (one-time purchase) or have GeoWimble Pro, the app no longer shows banners or full-screen ads. Rewarded videos stay optional and only appear if you ask for them.

5. Purchases

Remove ads (a one-time purchase) and GeoWimble Pro (a monthly subscription) are processed by Google Play. The price you see always comes from Google Play for your country. We receive no payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device which purchases you own and remembers the answer on your device. You can restore purchases in Settings > Restore purchases. You manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to request ads, to load the history photos and to talk to Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above.

7. Children

GeoWimble is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information that Google processes for ads or purchases, Google is responsible; you can use the privacy controls in your Google account and in the consent dialog. If you are in the EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Users in the United States. If you live in a US state with a privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas), you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data for targeted advertising, to know what is collected, and to request deletion or correction. We do not sell personal data ourselves and do not hold personal data about you. Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob; where required, the app shows a privacy choices message (via Google's User Messaging Platform) in which you can choose "Don't sell or share my data". Removing ads with the one-time purchase stops banners and full-screen ads. We do not discriminate against you for using these choices.

9. Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Connections to Google, NASA and the Library of Congress use HTTPS.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will show it in the app and update the date above.
