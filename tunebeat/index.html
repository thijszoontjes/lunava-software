Projects / Tunebeat

Tuner & metronome · Android

Tune fast. Keep perfect time.

Tunebeat is a fast, accurate instrument tuner and a rock-steady metronome in one clean app. Tune guitar, bass, ukulele, violin and more, then practise with a metronome that stays in time. No account, no sign-up, and both work fully offline.

Get Tunebeat on Google Play ↗ Back to all projects

No account · Audio is analysed on your phone and never stored

Built for the practice room, not for the paywall.

Open the app and play. Auto mode finds the closest string by itself, a smooth needle and a clear cents readout show which way to turn the peg, and a precision mode slows the needle down when you want to tune to the cent.

The tuner and the metronome are free. Ads are shown in the free version, and you can remove them for good with a one-time purchase or with Pro.

01

30+ tunings, and your own

Guitar (6, 7 and 12 string), bass, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin and banjo, from Standard to DADGAD. Build a custom tuning with up to 8 strings.

02

A tuner you can read at a glance

Auto and manual string modes, flats or sharps, Do-Re-Mi note names, an adjustable A4 reference pitch, a reference tone and microphone sensitivity.

03

A metronome that holds

40 to 208 BPM, tap tempo, time signatures from 2/4 to 12/8 with subdivisions, flash and vibration, a tempo trainer and a setlist of saved tempos.

Your microphone, your phone.

Tunebeat listens through the microphone only while the tuner screen is open. Audio is analysed on your phone and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Settings stay on your device, and there is no account.

The free version shows ads, with a consent choice where your region requires one. Read the privacy policy for the details. The screenshots above are rendered from the app.

Free to use. Pro if you want more.

The tuner and the metronome never depend on Pro. Pro adds a few extras and removes all ads.

FREE · ALWAYS

The full tuner and metronome

  • 30+ tunings, custom tunings, precision mode and Do-Re-Mi note names.
  • Metronome with tap tempo, time signatures, subdivisions and tempo trainer.
  • Six click sounds, flash and vibration, and a setlist of 5 saved tempos.
  • Ads in the free version. Optionally watch a short ad for 24 ad-free hours.

TUNEBEAT PRO · MONTHLY

More ways to practise

  • Polyrhythm metronome, for example 3 against 4.
  • Four extra click sounds and a setlist of 30 saved tempos.
  • No ads at all while Pro is active.
  • Cancel any time in Google Play. A separate one-time Remove ads purchase is also available.
