Tunebeat: Tuner & Metronome · Android app

Privacy policy

This policy describes how the current version of Tunebeat uses your microphone, what it stores on your device, and which services it uses for ads and purchases.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Your audio never leaves your phone.

The tuner analyses sound from the microphone while the tuner screen is open. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Tunebeat has no account. It uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for the optional Pro subscription and the Remove ads purchase.

Who provides the app?

Tunebeat: Tuner & Metronome is provided by Lunava Software, operated by Thijs Zoontjes in the Netherlands (KVK 42195099). This policy applies to the Android app. Website visits are covered by the website privacy and analytics notice.

Microphone

Tunebeat asks for the microphone permission so the tuner can find the pitch of the note you play. The sound is analysed on your phone in small pieces while the tuner screen is open, and each piece is discarded right after the pitch has been calculated. Tunebeat does not record audio, does not save it, and does not upload it. The tuner stops listening when you leave the screen or send the app to the background. The metronome does not use the microphone. You can deny or withdraw the permission in your device settings; the metronome and the settings keep working without it.

Information stored on your device

Tunebeat stores the following in the app's local storage so it can remember your choices:

  • your instrument, tuning (including a custom tuning you create), reference pitch, note-name and precision settings;
  • your metronome tempo, time signature, sound, trainer and polyrhythm settings;
  • your setlist (the tempo names and settings you save);
  • the time of your last successful Pro or Remove ads check with Google Play, and the end time of any temporary ad-free or trial period you earned by watching a rewarded video.

This information is not uploaded to Lunava Software or any Lunava server, and it is not used for advertising or analytics. The app does not ask for your name, email address, account, location, contacts, photos or camera. The app turns Android's automatic cloud backup off for its data.

Ads, purchases and third parties

Tunebeat shows ads from Google AdMob: banner ads, an inline banner below the tuner controls, occasional full-screen ads when you leave the metronome, and optional rewarded videos. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the Android advertising ID, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information and other device or app identifiers to serve, measure and protect ads. Data practices can vary with your device, region and settings. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Tunebeat uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form where Google requires one, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and a privacy-options entry point in Settings when required. Ads are not requested until the consent state allows it. On Android 13 and later you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Tunebeat offers two optional purchases through Google Play Billing: Tunebeat Pro, a monthly subscription that removes ads and unlocks extra features, and Remove ads, a one-time purchase. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Lunava does not receive your payment card details and has no server that holds your purchases; the app checks your purchase status with Google Play on your device. Pro renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

The app needs an internet connection only for ads, the consent form and purchase checks. Tunebeat has no separate analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no user accounts. Google Play, Android and your email provider may process information when you use those services or contact us.

Your choices and deleting data

You can change your ad privacy choices in the Settings screen of the app when Google requires that option for your region. You can buy Pro or Remove ads to stop all ads. To remove everything Tunebeat stored on your device, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. There is no account and no server-side profile to delete.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or to object to processing. You can also contact your data protection authority. Contact us using the address below. For data held by Google, you can also use the controls described in Google's privacy policy.

Retention and security

Settings, tunings and setlist stay on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to this local data; its protection depends on your device security and lock screen. Google keeps information it processes for ads, billing and platform services according to its own retention policies; see Google's privacy policy linked above.

Children's privacy

Tunebeat is a general-audience music tool and is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Material changes to data practices will be reflected here and in relevant app disclosures.
