Projects / Crimpnest

Climbing logbook · Android

Your climbing, logged privately.

Crimpnest is a climbing logbook for bouldering and roping. Log every session and climb, watch your level grow and keep the whole history on your phone, with no account, no sign-up and no cloud.

Get Crimpnest on Google Play ↗ Back to all projects

No account · Stays on your device · Works offline · Free with ads

Tap in a climb. Keep the history.

Start a session at the gym, at the crag or on your home wall, pick the grade and how it went, and move on to the next problem. Crimpnest keeps the session, the grades and the notes together, so a season of climbing stays easy to look back on.

There is nothing to set up and no account to create. Your logbook lives on your phone, and you can export a backup file or your climbs as CSV whenever you like.

01

Log like a climber

Boulder and route grades in V-scale, Font, French, YDS, UIAA and Ewbank, converted between systems. Styles that mean something: onsight, flash, send, repeat and attempt, for bouldering, sport, top rope, trad and multipitch.

02

Level and progress

A level system built on your best climbs of the last year, separately for boulders and routes, with ten tiers from Warm-up to Apex. Grade pyramids, volume per week, style mix and trends. Trends are free, always.

03

Projects and places

Keep a list of projects with attempts, notes and the date you sent them, and mark the holds on a photo of your project. Save your gyms and crags and see what you climbed where.

04

Strength and gear

Log hangboard hangs, pull-ups and your own tests with personal records and a trend line. Track shoes, ropes, harnesses and chalk, with sessions, climbs and falls counted from your logs.

05

Goals and milestones

A weekly goal ring and week dots on Home keep you honest without nagging. Earn badges for first flashes, grade breakthroughs, streaks and more.

06

Smart little touches

Crimpnest warns you when you are about to log something you already sent and shows how it went last time. A built-in rest timer covers hard tries and the pauses between sets.

Private by design.

There is no account and no server: your logbook is stored on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where it is required, and the Remove ads purchase goes through Google Play.

Good to know: levels and milestones are a fun way to follow your own progress, not an official grade or ranking. The sample data in the screenshots is made up.
