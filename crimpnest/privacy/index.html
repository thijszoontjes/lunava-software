Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what happens with information when you use Crimpnest. Lunava Software (sole proprietorship, KvK 42195099), Weg acker 47, 1965 TG Heemskerk, the Netherlands is responsible for the app. Contact: lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your climbing logbook (sessions, climbs, projects, places, strength and gear entries, notes, photos, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and we have no server or account system.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some browse screens and an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a task. Google may process some device information for that, after you give consent where the law requires it.
  • Nothing you log in the app is sent to advertisers.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your logbook is deleted. We cannot restore it. Export a backup if you want to keep a copy.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or have access to your name, email address, location, contacts, the climbs you log or your photos. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services in the app, and the app has no account or sign-in.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores on your device: sessions, climbs, projects, places, strength and gear entries, notes, photos of climbs and the marks you draw on them (kept in the app's private storage, not visible to other apps), your settings and, if you bought it, your ad-free status. You control this data. Delete single items in the app, use Settings > Delete all data, clear the app's data in Android, or uninstall the app to remove it.

3. Backups and exports you create

When you export a backup file or a CSV of your climbs, the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice. We do not receive it. Photos are not part of the backup file.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Crimpnest uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may use your advertising ID, IP address (for approximate location), device and app information to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Google acts under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Ads are shown only after the consent check allows it. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app first shows Google's consent form. You can reopen it at any time in Settings under Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ads are not shown while you log a climb or during a live session, and never to people who bought Remove ads. We do not give advertisers any content from your logbook.

5. Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details or name. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can restore it after a reinstall.

6. Permissions

The app asks for the internet connection (for ads and the purchase check). It does not ask for access to your camera, location, contacts, microphone or storage. Photos you add are chosen with the Android picker and copied into the app's private storage. The app can keep the screen awake during a live session if you turn that setting on.

7. Children

Crimpnest is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the app.

9. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information that Google processes for ads, Google is responsible; you can use the controls in your Google account and in the consent dialog. If you are in the EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you live in a US state with a privacy law you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal data ourselves. Where required, the app shows Google's privacy choices message. Removing ads with the one-time purchase stops all ad requests.

10. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are turned off on purpose, so the only copy outside the device is a backup file you create yourself. Enable a screen lock on your phone for extra protection.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will show it in the app and update the date above.
