The Last Train: Puzzle Mystery · Android app

Privacy policy

This policy describes what the current version of The Last Train stores on your device and which app services it uses.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Your game save is stored locally.

The game uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for purchases. Android may include local app data in a device backup. The game has no account or Lunava cloud save.

Who provides the app?

The Last Train: Puzzle Mystery is provided by Lunava Software, operated by Thijs Zoontjes in the Netherlands (KVK 42195099). This policy applies to the mobile game. It is separate from analytics that may be used on the Lunava Software website; website visits are covered by the website privacy and analytics notice.

Information stored on your device

The game uses local app storage to remember information needed to continue your game. This can include:

  • which story and puzzle levels you have opened or completed;
  • the number of free tips you have, when the next tip is due, and which tips you have used;
  • your Last Train Tickets balance and any locally recorded purchase status or transaction reference;
  • sound and music settings and the level currently shown.

This information is used to run the game and save your place. The current version does not upload this game data to Lunava Software or a game server. It is not used for advertising or analytics.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, account, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not record audio. Music, voice, sound and artwork are included with the app.

Network connections and third parties

The Android app uses Google AdMob to display occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. The app marks ad requests as child-directed and limits ad content to the general-audience (G) rating. Google says its child-directed setting disables interest-based and remarketing ads and prevents the Android advertising identifier from being transmitted. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process the device’s IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other device or app identifiers to serve, measure and protect ads. Google’s SDK disclosure says it may collect and share these data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data practices can vary with device, region, settings and applicable choices. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether Google requires a privacy form or privacy-options entry point for the user’s region and settings. The app displays forms and a Settings control when Google reports they are required; their availability depends on those requirements. The game also uses Google Play Billing for optional ticket packs and Remove Ads. Google processes the transaction and payment information under its own policies; Lunava does not receive your payment card details. The app stores purchase status and transaction references locally to deliver purchases.

The game does not use a separate game analytics service or send game progress or ticket balances to a Lunava server. Google Play, Android device backups, and your email provider may process information when you use those services or contact us.

Your choices and deleting game data

You can reset the story from the in-game Settings. This resets story progress and used-tip records; purchased or earned ticket balances and local purchase records are retained by the reset function. To remove local app data, uninstall the game or clear its storage in your device settings. Manage any device backup separately through your device or Google account settings. The game has no account and no server-side game profile to delete.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage can permanently remove your local progress. The game does not provide its own cross-device sync. Android device backup and restore may retain or restore app data depending on your device settings. Google Play may restore eligible purchases; consumed ticket packs are not guaranteed to be restored.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or to object to processing. You can also contact your data protection authority. Contact us using the address below; we may need enough information to understand and respond to your request. For data held by Google, you can also use the controls described in Google’s privacy policy.

Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until you reset it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The game keeps a backup copy of its save in the app's local storage to help recover from an interrupted or damaged save; the local copy is removed when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. A separate Android device backup may remain subject to your Google account and device settings.

The game does not add a separate encryption layer to this saved progress. Its protection depends on your device and operating system security, device lock and backup settings. Device-level backups may handle app data according to your platform settings and provider terms. Google retains information it processes for advertising, billing and platform services according to its own retention policies; see Google’s privacy policy linked above.

Children's privacy

The game is intended for children across the Google Play child age groups and does not require an account. Ad requests use child-directed treatment and the G content rating. The app includes advertising and optional purchases handled by Google as described above. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning a child.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Material changes to data practices will be reflected here and in relevant app disclosures.
