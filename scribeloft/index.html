Projects / Scribeloft

Novel writing studio · Android

Write the whole book.

Scribeloft is a focused studio for writing a novel on your phone or tablet. Plan it with story structures and a plot grid, keep your characters and world in a codex, write in a calm editor and compile a real manuscript. No account, and your book stays on your device.

No account · Your manuscript stays on your device

A novel is a lot of moving parts.

Chapters, scenes, characters, places, storylines, drafts. Scribeloft keeps them together, so you can move from the first idea to a finished manuscript without juggling several apps. It works on a phone in a queue and on a tablet at a desk, with a reference panel beside the editor when there is room.

Scribeloft is free to use and shows ads outside the editor. You can remove them with a one-time purchase, or go Pro for the tools that help with a long book.

01

A calm place to write

A distraction-free editor with headings, quotes and scene breaks, focus mode, writing sprints and a word-count goal for every scene, chapter and book. There are no ads in the editor.

02

Plan the whole book

A binder with parts, chapters and scenes, plus corkboard and outline views, a plot grid that shows which storylines run through which scenes, and story structure templates such as three acts, Save the Cat and the hero’s journey.

03

Codex and series bible

Keep characters, places, items and lore in one codex, linked to the scenes where they appear. For a series, share the same characters and world across every book.

04

Light writing checks

Spot repeated words, filler words, long sentences and adverbs, with a readability score for each scene. The checks run on your phone and nothing is uploaded.

05

Rewrite without fear

Save a snapshot of a scene before you change it, compare the versions side by side and restore the old text with one tap. Daily goals, streaks and a projected finish date keep you moving.

06

Export a real manuscript

Compile to plain text, Markdown and a simple PDF for free, or to Word, EPUB and a manuscript-format PDF. Share a Word file with comments for your editor. Import text, Markdown and Word files.

Screens show sample data.

Your book, your phone.

Your manuscript lives on your device. We never receive it, and there is no account to create. Back it up to a single file in any folder you choose and restore it whenever you like. With Pro you can also sync between your phone and tablet through a folder you already use, such as one handled by Google Drive or Syncthing. Scribeloft has no server of its own.

The app is available in English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish, following your system language. The free version shows banner ads on the library, progress and settings screens and a full-screen video ad when you export, with a consent choice where your region requires one.
