Scribeloft: Novel Writing · Android app

Privacy policy

This policy describes what the current version of Scribeloft stores on your device, what it can send out, and which services it uses for ads and purchases.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Your manuscript stays on your device.

Scribeloft has no account and no Lunava server. Your books, notes, codex and snapshots are stored locally in the app. Nothing you write is uploaded by the app. It uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play Billing for the optional Pro subscription and the Remove Ads purchase.

Who provides the app?

Scribeloft: Novel Writing is provided by Lunava Software, operated by Thijs Zoontjes in the Netherlands (KVK 42195099). This policy applies to the Android app. Website visits are covered by the website privacy and analytics notice.

Information stored on your device

Scribeloft stores the following in the app's local storage:

  • your books and everything in them: scenes, chapters, parts, research notes, synopses, codex entries, plot grid, snapshots and word counts;
  • your writing statistics, such as daily word counts used for streaks, pace and the heatmap;
  • your settings, such as theme, font size, goals and reminder time;
  • the time of your last successful Pro or Remove Ads check with Google Play, and the end time of any temporary Pro export access you earned by watching a rewarded video.

This information is not uploaded to Lunava Software or any Lunava server and is not used for advertising or analytics. The app does not ask for your name, email address, account, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

The app allows Android's automatic backup. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may include the app's local data (your books and settings) in your own Google backup or a device-to-device transfer, under Google's terms and your Google account settings. Lunava does not receive or have access to that backup.

Export, backup and import

You choose when to export. Scribeloft writes TXT, Markdown, PDF, Word (.docx), EPUB and full-backup files to a location you pick with Android's file picker, or hands them to another app you pick in the share sheet. Once a file leaves the app, it is under the control of the place or app you chose. Pro can also write automatic backup files to a folder you select. Importing reads only the files you select. The app cannot read other files on your device.

Ads, purchases and third parties

Scribeloft shows ads from Google AdMob: banner ads on the Library, Progress and Settings screens, and an optional rewarded video on the Export screen that unlocks Pro exports for a limited time. There are no ads in the editor and no full-screen ads that interrupt writing. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the Android advertising ID, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information and other device or app identifiers to serve, measure and protect ads. Data practices can vary with your device, region and settings. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Scribeloft uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form where Google requires one, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and a privacy-options entry point in Settings when required. Ads are not requested until the consent state allows it. On Android 13 and later you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Scribeloft offers two optional purchases through Google Play Billing: Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, and Scribeloft Pro, a subscription that also unlocks extra features. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Lunava does not receive your payment card details and has no server that holds your purchases; the app checks your purchase status with Google Play on your device. A subscription renews until you cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

The app needs an internet connection only for ads, the consent form and purchase checks. Scribeloft has no separate analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no user accounts. Google Play, Android and your email provider may process information when you use those services or contact us.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily writing reminder, Scribeloft asks for the notification permission on Android 13 and later and schedules one local notification per day at the time you choose. The reminder is created on your device and contains no data from your manuscript. You can turn it off in Settings or withdraw the permission in your device settings.

Your choices and deleting data

You can change your ad privacy choices in the Settings screen of the app when Google requires that option for your region. You can buy Remove Ads or Pro to stop ads. You can delete a book inside the app. To remove everything Scribeloft stored on your device, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Export files and backups that you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them. There is no account and no server-side profile to delete.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or to object to processing. You can also contact your data protection authority. Contact us using the address below. For data held by Google, you can also use the controls described in Google's privacy policy.

Retention and security

Your books and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to this local data; its protection depends on your device security and lock screen. Google keeps information it processes for ads, billing, backup and platform services according to its own retention policies.

Children's privacy

Scribeloft is a general-audience writing tool and is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Material changes to data practices will be reflected here and in relevant app disclosures.
