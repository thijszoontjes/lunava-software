Cardwren · Android app

Privacy policy

This notice explains what Cardwren stores on your device and what information is handled by the Google services the app uses.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Your sets stay on your device.

Cardwren needs no account and runs no server that stores or syncs your flashcards. Google still processes limited information to show ads and handle the Remove Ads purchase.

The short version

Cardwren works offline and without an account. Your sets, pictures, study progress, history and settings stay on your device. We run no server and no analytics, and Cardwren has no crash reporting of its own, so we receive none of your data. The only outside parties are Google (ads and purchases), described below.

What stays on your device

  • The flashcard and quiz sets you make or import, including pictures you add.
  • Your attempts, scores, study schedule, stars and settings.
  • An optional daily reminder, which is a local notification set by your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

You can delete all of it at any time with Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. You can also make a backup file and restore it yourself.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may process your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information, how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics and crash information from the ad SDK, under Google's privacy policy and the rules for how Google uses data from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, Cardwren first shows Google's consent form (Google UMP) and requests ads only after you have answered. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy choices where that row is shown, or in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Cardwren does not receive the data Google collects.

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time purchase "Remove Ads" is handled entirely by Google Play. Cardwren only learns whether you own the product, so it can hide the ads. We never see your name, card or other payment details. Once you own it, Cardwren makes no ad requests.

Sharing sets

When you share a set, you choose how: a link, a QR code or a file. A link holds the set inside the part of the address after the "#". Browsers do not send that part to any server. The web page that opens such a link is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of the page request, but not the part after the "#". Whoever you give a link or file to can read the set, so only share what you are happy to share.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads and purchases.
  • Notifications: only for the daily reminder, only if you turn it on.
  • Vibration: for tap feedback, which you can turn off.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob in the free version.

Children

Cardwren is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up or delete. For data Google processes, use Google's own tools and controls. If you have a question or complaint, email us. You may also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and the version in the app store listing.
