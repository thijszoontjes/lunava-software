Projects / Cardwren

Flashcards and quizzes · Android

Study smarter. Keep it private.

Cardwren is a flashcard and quiz maker that lives on your phone. Build your own sets, practise with 13 kinds of questions, let spaced repetition schedule your reviews and share a set with a link. No account, no server, works without internet.

Works offline · No account · Free, with optional Remove Ads

Your sets, your phone.

Cardwren keeps everything on your device: sets, pictures and progress. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is uploaded, so you can study on the train, on a plane or without a data plan.

Mix question types in one set, paste a list from your notes to get flashcards in seconds, and let the app show each card just before you would forget it.

01

13 ways to quiz yourself

Multiple choice, true or false, typed answers, picture choice, ordering, matching pairs, tap the spot on a picture, fill in the blanks, numbers, sorting, labelling, listening and classic flashcards.

02

Spaced repetition

Flashcards come back at the right moment. Rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy and see mastery and a 7-day forecast per set.

03

Learn, Test and Match

Learn repeats what you miss until it sticks, Test builds a fresh practice round, and Match lets you race the clock pairing terms and definitions.

04

Make sets fast

Paste a list from a spreadsheet, add pictures, explanations and time limits, export a set as CSV and back up everything with one file.

05

Share without a server

Send a set as a link, a QR code or a file. The set travels inside the link, and the other person previews it before adding it to their library.

06

Streaks and progress

Set a daily goal, keep your streak, see your scores over time and get an optional daily reminder. Light and dark mode follow your phone, and tablets get a roomier layout.

Screens show the built-in demo sets.

Private by design.

Cardwren has no account and no server of its own. Your sets and progress stay on your device. The app is free and shows a small banner ad on the browsing screens, never during a quiz. The ads are served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where your region requires one. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use for the details.
