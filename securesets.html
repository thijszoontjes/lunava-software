No account required · Your training data stays on your device

A plan built around real life.

Choose your goal, how often you train, the equipment you have and the time you can give a session. Exclude training splits you do not want. Start from an editable routine, build your own or use the optional on-device AI model to generate a plan.

Download the optional model once and workout generation runs on your phone, even offline. While training, SecureSets suggests your previous weights and reps, keeps unfinished sessions resumable and puts your workout history in one place.

FREE · CORE TRAINING TOOLS

Plan and log your workouts

  • Build, edit and organize routines with the exercise library or your own exercises.
  • Log sets, reps and weights, with previous performance and a built-in rest timer.
  • Keep session history, resume unfinished workouts and review session recaps.
  • Use starter routines, CSV import, an estimated 1RM calculator and JSON backup/restore.

The free app includes ads. Workout data is stored locally and no account is required.

SECURESETS PRO

Know what to work on next

  • Progression guidance: set a rep range and weight step for each exercise; see a suggested next step and why.
  • Training blocks: plan 1–16 weeks, set goals, and move or skip sessions as your week changes.
  • Equipment profiles: swap in a similar exercise for the gym, home or a hotel setup.
  • Deeper progress: review sets by muscle group, estimated personal bests and lift trends.
  • Ad-free while Pro is active, with the Rose theme included.

Choose monthly or lifetime access in the app. Google Play shows the current price before purchase.
