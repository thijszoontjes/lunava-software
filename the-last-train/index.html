Two journeys · Twelve chapters · Sixty cases

Two journeys. Sixty cases. One vanished train.

Step into a world of rainy platforms, lamplit corridors and curious passengers. Inspector Finch and his companion Pippin are ready to follow every lead — and they need your eye for detail.

Each stop brings a new kind of puzzle. Connect stars, redirect beams of light, sort mixed-up flasks and search the carriages for clues. Take your time, ask Finch for a hint when you need one and see where the tracks lead.
