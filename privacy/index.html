Privacy & analytics

This page explains the analytics used on lunavasoftware.nl. SecureSets and Sunrise Alarm have separate privacy information for their apps; see their respective product pages.

Who operates this website?

Lunava Software is operated by Thijs Zoontjes in the Netherlands (KVK 42195099). For questions about this website or its analytics, email lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited and how the website is used. If you accept analytics, the Google tag for measurement ID G-8890ZF2NTH loads. It can send page and interaction measurements along with technical information such as browser and device details to Google. Google processes information under its Privacy Policy.

Analytics is off by default. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag does not load and this website does not send visit measurements to Google Analytics. The website code grants analytics storage only after you accept and denies the advertising-related consent options. It does not add Google Ads tags.

Your choice

When browser storage is available, your choice is saved in this browser so the banner does not appear on every page. If you accept, the Google tag receives that analytics permission and sends visit measurements. The choice may not carry between browsers or devices, and clearing browser storage will show the banner again.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time. If you withdraw after accepting, the website stops sending future analytics measurements, removes Google Analytics cookies it can access for this site, and reloads the page. Information already sent to Google may remain in the Analytics property.
