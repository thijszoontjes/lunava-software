Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what happens with information when you use Pagevane. Lunava Software (sole proprietorship, KvK 42195099), Weg acker 47, 1965 TG Heemskerk, the Netherlands is responsible for the app. Contact: lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your device. Converting, previewing, text recognition (OCR), signing, form filling and password protection all happen on your phone. We never receive your files, their names or their content.
  • Pagevane has no account, no server and no cloud storage.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process some device information for that, after you give consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive no payment details.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or have access to your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or files. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. The text recognition languages are bundled in the app, so no document is ever sent anywhere for recognition.

2. Information stored on your device

Finished results and their names are kept in the History list on your device so you can open, rename, save or share them again. A drawn signature can be remembered for next time, and your settings (such as theme and OCR language) are stored. You can delete any history item or all of it in the app. Clearing the app's data in Android or uninstalling the app removes everything. Pagevane only reads a file when you pick it.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA/UK; Google LLC elsewhere). Ads appear as a banner at the bottom of the screen and as an occasional full-screen ad after a few finished conversions, only on the home screen. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address and information about how you interact with ads. Google's own policy explains this: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Your documents are never shared with advertisers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the app no longer shows ads, including the banner and full-screen ads.

4. Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We receive no payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device whether you own the purchase and remembers the answer on your device. You can restore purchases in Settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to request ads and to talk to Google Play. Your documents are not sent over the network.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above. It is not used if you have removed ads.

Pagevane asks for no permission for contacts, location, camera or microphone.

6. Children

Pagevane is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information that Google processes for ads, Google is responsible; you can use the privacy controls in your Google account and in the consent dialog. If you are in the EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your national data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). For any question, write to lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

Users in the United States. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising, to know what is collected, and to request deletion or correction. We do not sell personal data and do not hold personal data about you. For ad data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls.

8. Security

Your documents, history and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Password protection uses AES-256 encryption performed on your device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will show it in the app and update the date above.
