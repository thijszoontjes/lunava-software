Projects / Pagevane

Document toolkit · Android

Every document tool, on your phone.

Pagevane merges, splits, signs, scans and converts PDFs and other documents without sending them anywhere. Your files are processed on the device itself, with no upload, no account, no watermark and no daily limit.

No account · Files stay on your device · No watermark · Free with ads

Your documents never leave your phone.

Most online PDF tools ask you to upload your files first. Pagevane does the work on the device, so contracts, invoices and scans are not sent to a server, and there is no account to create.

Pick a tool, choose your files, check the preview and save or share the result. Everything you finish is kept in History on the device, where you can rename it, open it again or delete it.

01

PDF tools

Merge PDFs in the order you choose, split by page ranges or every N pages, and extract, delete, rotate and reorder pages with tappable thumbnails. Add page numbers or a text watermark, compress a PDF for email, or turn a PDF into images or text.

02

Protect and sign

Add a password to a PDF (AES-256) or remove it when you know it. Draw your signature, add text or a date and place them on any page, and fill in PDF forms.

03

Scan with OCR

Turn scans and photos into searchable PDFs or plain text. It works offline, in English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish.

04

Make a PDF

Create a PDF from photos or text, or from Markdown, HTML, Word and Excel files.

05

Convert

PDF to Word keeps text and headings. Word to Markdown, text or HTML, and CSV, JSON and Excel in every direction.

06

Made for phones

Preview before you save, rename the result and find it again in History. Light and dark mode.

Calm by design.

There is no account and no sign-up, and your files are not uploaded. Ads are served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where it is required, and the purchase goes through Google Play.

Good to know: Word, Excel, Markdown and HTML to PDF aim for clean, readable output, not an exact copy of Word's layout. PDF to Word keeps text and headings, not pictures or columns. Signatures are drawn images, not certified digital signatures.
