Projects / Packwick

Cozy arena fighter · Android

Read the enemy. Win the arena.

Packwick is a hand-drawn sword-and-sandals arena game for your phone. Every fight is a quick turn-based duel: see what your opponent is about to do, then strike, guard and counter, or unleash your weapon’s Special. No account, no internet needed, no gore.

Get Packwick on Google Play ↗ Back to all projects

Fully offline · No account · Free to play

A fight you can read.

Reading your opponent matters more than tapping fast. Before every move the enemy shows what it is about to do, so you can pick the right answer: a Strike, a Power hit, a Guard that counters, Focus to recover stamina, or your weapon’s Special. A fight takes a minute or two, which makes Packwick easy to play in short sessions.

Climb through five arenas, collect and forge better gear, and see how far the endless Gauntlet takes you.

01

Spot the intent

See whether the enemy is going for a heavy hit, a guard or a charge, and answer with a well-timed Guard and counter.

02

Manage your stamina

Big moves cost more stamina and Focus brings it back. Every turn is a small decision between pressure and patience.

03

25 fights, 5 arenas

Each arena ends in a champion, and the endless Gauntlet follows. Conditions such as Scorching Sun and Slippery Sand change from fight to fight.

04

Gear up and forge

Collect weapons, shields, helmets and armour across five tiers, upgrade them at the Forge and train Strength, Vitality, Agility and Endurance.

05

A camp to explore

Walk around the camp and visit the Armory, Forge, Training Yard, Healer and Laurel Shrine. Daily deals bring a new selection every day.

06

Look the part

Unlock outfits and nine cape colours, claim daily quests and a 7-day login reward, earn achievements and fill the monster bestiary.

Offline, on your device.

Packwick has no account and no server of its own. Your progress is stored on your device and you do not need an internet connection to play. The game is free to play and shows ads served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where your region requires one. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Packwick is rated PEGI 7 on Google Play for mild cartoon violence.

Free to play. Fair to buy.

Everything can be earned in the game. Google Play shows the price for your country before you buy.
