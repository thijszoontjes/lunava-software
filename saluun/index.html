A CLOSER LOOK

Everything in its place.

See the day at a glance, manage every appointment and give clients a smooth path to book.

Built around the way your salon works.

Share your salon's own booking link on your website or social channels. Clients can book without creating an account, choosing the service, stylist and time that suit them.

Set up your team, service categories, prices and working hours yourself. Adjust availability for each team member and block out holidays or one-off closing days when plans change.

A selected time is held briefly while a client completes their booking, then availability is checked again before confirmation. Clients pay at the salon, so you can offer online booking without adding online payments.
