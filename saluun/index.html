Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

How do I get started with Saluun?

Contact Thijs to discuss the setup for your salon and agree on a start date together.

What happens after I get in touch?

Your message goes to Thijs at Lunava Software. He’ll follow up to learn about your salon and discuss how Saluun fits the way you work. You’ll agree on a start date, price, VAT and terms in advance.

Do clients need an account to book?

No. Clients can choose a service, stylist and available time, then enter their name and email to confirm. Payment takes place at the salon.

Which languages can I use?

Dutch is the default language. You can switch the dashboard to English, and client communications are available in either Dutch or English.

How much does Saluun cost?

€44.95 per salon, per month, excluding VAT. Including 21% Dutch VAT, the total is €54.39 per month.
