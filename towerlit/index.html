Projects / Towerlit

Casual tower-stacking game · Android

Stack it. Light it up.

Towerlit is a cozy one-tap tower-stacking game. Time your tap, drop each floor onto the tower below and watch the windows light up as your skyline climbs higher than ever. Free to play, with no account.

Get Towerlit on Google Play ↗ Back to all projects

No account · Works offline · Progress stays on your device

One tap. One more floor.

A block slides across the screen. Tap to drop it and anything that overhangs gets sliced off, so every floor makes the next one a little harder. Land a Perfect to keep your full width and start a combo, and chain Perfects to watch your tower repair itself.

Every floor adds a lit window with its own tiny story. Runs are short, so it fits a minute on the bus as well as a long evening of chasing your best.

01

Simple to learn

One tap is all it takes. Tap to drop the sliding block, aim carefully and build as high as you can. There is nothing to read before you play.

02

Perfects repair your tower

Land a Perfect to keep your full width and start a combo. Chain Perfects and lost width grows back, so a good streak can save a shaky tower.

03

Two ways to play

Classic is one tower, as high as you can. Tower Series is three towers in a row: the heights add up to one big score and a bonus.

04

Make it yours

Collect building styles and skies, from cozy cottages to night skylines. Use boosters such as Steady, Wide and Magnet for a calmer climb.

05

A reason to come back

Finish daily missions and open a daily gift to build a streak. Everything in the shop can also be earned by playing.

06

Know your progress

The Statistics screen shows games played, your record, average floors, perfect drops and your best Tower Series total.

Calm by design.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress is stored on your device, and the game works offline. Ads are served by Google AdMob, with a consent choice where it is required, and purchases go through Google Play.

Ads are kept out of the way: optional reward ads, a short ad only after a finished run and banners on menu screens, never during play.
