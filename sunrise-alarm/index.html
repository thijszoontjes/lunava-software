No account · Sleep estimates are processed on your phone

Light first. No subscription required.

Set a wake-up time and let the screen move gradually from dark to a warm sunrise. Make separate alarms for different days, choose a sunrise duration and sound, or run the 20-second demo before you decide.

The light alarm, snooze and an independent back-up sound stay free. A separate back-up alarm is scheduled for five minutes after wake-up if the alarm has not been stopped.

Optional sleep insights, estimated on your phone.

Turn on sleep tracking when you want it. Sunrise Alarm uses the phone microphone during Sleep Mode to estimate awake, light, deep and REM stages, time asleep, breathing rate and a sleep score with a clear breakdown.

Raw audio is never stored or uploaded. If the signal is not good enough, the app explains why instead of showing made-up stages or a score. These estimates are for personal insight, not a medical measurement.
