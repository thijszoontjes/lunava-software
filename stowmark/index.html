Home inventory & warranty tracker · Android

Know what you own.

Stowmark is a private home inventory that lives on your phone. Build it room by room, keep the receipt, the serial number and the warranty date with every item, and get a reminder before a warranty ends. No account, no cloud, no sign-up.

No account · Your home inventory stays on your phone

Everything an insurer or a repair shop could ask for.

When something breaks or goes missing, the hard part is rarely the item itself. It is finding the receipt, the serial number and the date the warranty ends. Stowmark keeps all of that next to the photo of the item, in the room where it lives.

It is free to use and shows ads. A one-time Remove ads purchase removes them for good, and every feature stays free either way.

01

Room by room, shelf by shelf

Nested places such as house, room, shelf and box. See per room what is complete and what still needs a photo, a receipt, a price or a serial number.

02

Receipts and serial numbers

Photograph a receipt and Stowmark suggests the store, date and total on your phone. You check every field before you save. Serial plates can be photographed or scanned as a barcode.

03

Reminders that matter

Warnings before a warranty ends, a service is due or a return window closes, plus expiry dates for paint, batteries and medicine. A Needs attention list and a home-screen widget show what is next.

04

QR labels for boxes and shelves

Print a sheet of QR stickers and scan one to jump straight to the item or the place. The code holds only an ID, never your data.

05

Ready for insurance and moving

A PDF home inventory report grouped by room, a one-item claim PDF, CSV export, a ZIP of all photos, and CSV import with column mapping from Excel, Sheets or Numbers.

06

Value, room by room

Purchase price, value today and a total per room. It is a simple planning estimate, not an appraisal. Add your own fields too, such as paint code, size or IMEI.

Your home, your phone.

Your home inventory, photos and receipts stay on your device. We never receive them, and there is no account to create. Photos are saved without GPS location data, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Back up everything to a single .stowmark file, optionally encrypted with a passphrase of your own, and restore it on another phone without any cloud. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your inventory is deleted, so Stowmark reminds you to make a backup.

The free version shows ads, with a consent choice where your region requires one. Read the privacy policy and the terms for the details.
