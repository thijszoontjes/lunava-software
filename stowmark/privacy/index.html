Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what happens with information when you use Stowmark. Lunava Software (sole proprietorship, KvK 42195099), Weg acker 47, 1965 TG Heemskerk, the Netherlands is responsible for the app. Contact: lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your inventory (items, photos, receipts, notes, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and we have no server or account system.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process some device information for that, after you give consent where the law requires it.
  • Nothing you store in your inventory is sent to advertisers.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your data is deleted. We cannot restore it.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or have access to your name, email address, location, contacts, the items you list, your photos, your receipts or your backup files. We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores on your device: the items and rooms you create, their photos and documents (in the app's private storage, not visible to other apps), your settings, reminder state and, if you bought it, your ad-free status. Photos you add are re-saved by the app as new image files, which removes location data (GPS) that the original photo may have contained.

You control this data. Delete a single item, empty "Recently deleted", use Settings > Delete all data, clear the app's data in Android, or uninstall the app to remove it.

3. Backups and exports you create

When you create a backup or export (".stowmark", PDF, CSV, ZIP), the file is written to the location you choose using Android's file picker. It may contain everything in your inventory. We do not receive it. Where it goes afterwards, for example a cloud drive, is up to you and is covered by that provider's policy. Backups can be encrypted with a passphrase you choose (AES-256-GCM). We cannot recover a lost passphrase.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA/UK; Google LLC elsewhere). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app version and interactions with ads. This is described in Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy. In other regions, ads may be requested as allowed by local law; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

If you buy "Remove Ads", the app no longer starts the ads software and requests no ads.

5. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive no payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device whether you own "Remove Ads" and remembers the answer on your device.

6. Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode into the serial number field, the app uses the Google code scanner, part of Google Play services. Scanning happens on your device; the camera image is not sent to us. The scanner does not need camera permission for the app.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): to show warranty and service reminders. Optional.
  • Camera: the app opens your camera app to take a photo and receives only the resulting picture. It does not request camera permission itself.
  • Photos: you choose pictures with the system photo picker; the app sees only what you select.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: if you enable app lock, the app asks Android to verify you. The app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

8. Children

Stowmark is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information that Google processes for ads, Google is responsible; you can use the privacy controls in your Google account and in the consent dialog. If you are in the EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. App backups to Google Drive and device-to-device transfer by Android are turned off on purpose, so the only copy outside the device is a backup file you create yourself. Enable a screen lock on your phone, and enable app lock in Settings for extra protection.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will show it in the app and update the date above.
